# ITER presentation: motion revision

## Scope

The revision preserves the accepted navigator and its fictional building graph. The presentation uses one persistent Three.js canvas; GSAP controls a numeric camera, floor and route pose. Navigation retargets from current values and cancels obsolete callbacks. No routing algorithm or room measurement is generated by AI.

A 14-second publicly available motion reference was studied for changes in scale, side and negative space: https://youtube.com/shorts/96Tq7h6XQ4w. Its branding and imagery are not reused.

One Kling 4.0 generation through Higgsfield, job `4553f75e-c902-4cd6-b85e-10f1eb1e6ab7`, was quoted at 19.5 credits with both actual start/end renders before submission. Output: 1920 x 1080, 24 fps, 145 frames, 6.04 seconds. The runtime copy has no audio and uses faststart; the compressed video stream was copied without re-encoding. The original, source frames, artistic prompt, parameters and sanitized provenance are preserved in the repository.

The six sampled frames retain the model's silhouette and principal architectural elements. Side-by-side 1920 x 1080 endpoint comparisons show matching contours, pose and scale with somewhat softer generated shading. Empty video edges are softly masked; architecture is not hidden to disguise a geometry mismatch. The intro is ornamental, not evidence of route computation.

## First-slice implementation checks

- Intro is skippable by button, Escape or normal scene navigation. It does not replay on return. Reduced motion bypasses it.
- A stable first-frame poster appears before media playback. A failed or delayed video does not block navigation. Hidden playback stops.
- Cancellation guards prevent React StrictMode cleanup from treating an obsolete play promise as a new playback failure.
- The building changes side and turns 90 degrees, then moves to individual staircase, door and lift details.
- Main door 204 is 105 cm in graph data. The separate 72 cm passage is not mislabeled as this door. Geometry is schematic, not a visual measurement.
- Conditions form remains available as a secondary disclosure. Route distance appears after explicit calculation or the story's completed route reveal.
- 8 focused intro tests passed after final first-paint, cancellation and faststart changes. Type checking and focused lint passed.

Independent first-slice evidence and subsequent regression findings are recorded below. A successful build alone is not motion acceptance.

## Expanded review corrections

The full-size review found a CSS/GSAP conflict at 1536 x 864 that numerical model bounds alone did not catch: a pixel translation inherited from CSS was combined with a second percentage translation. The canvas moved under the header even though the model fitted its internal projection. Presentation composition now explicitly clears residual pixel x/y before setting its percentage transform. The actual 3D poses remain unchanged.

Fallback transitions now apply complete static host, text and route-metadata states without waiting for a WebGL controller. Both initial WebGL unavailability and context loss during motion were exercised. Image readiness is included in the settled-state contract, and phone captures have intrinsic dimensions, preventing verification or recording of an un-decoded image. Navigation remains available during loading.

Static route and detour posters use the real renderer at zoom 0.76 instead of the live 0.82, with the original pose restored immediately after capture. This provides a generous print/fallback margin without changing live composition or graph data. PDF-only rules remove an accidental scroll/clipping constraint and keep the distance unit separate from the lift caption.

The complete unit suite passed: 184 tests across 17 files. Production compilation and full lint also passed after the fallback, image-readiness and PDF fixes. Final public verification is performed separately from compilation.
